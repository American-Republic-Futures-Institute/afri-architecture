## First material reconciliation: VA Board of Contract Appeals

The exact original retained 38 U.S.C. §301(c)(7), OLRC release through Pub. L. 119-124 (October 15, 2026), still lists a *Board of Contract Appeals* in VA's enumerated composition.

Separately, the official [Civilian Board of Contract Appeals history](https://cbca.gov/board/) states the CBCA was established January 6, 2007 and consolidated predecessor contract-dispute functions, including VA's. A separately identifiable official codified [41 U.S.C. §7105](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t41/s7105/) locates the Civilian Board within the General Services Administration and provides distinct statutory jurisdiction. Its code edition differs from the cached VA edition.

This is a **legal-text-versus-operational-history reconciliation problem**, not permission to keep rendering VA BCA as an active 2026 operating component, to move CBCA organizationally into VA, or to claim a complete identity succession. Original independent publisher and 41 USC HTTP response bytes have **not** yet been archived; recorded SHA-256/Git blob fields are deliberately **null**. Adjudication must examine Public Law 109-163 §847, applicable termination/transfer provisions and remaining historic or other decision functions, qualified by a reviewer.
