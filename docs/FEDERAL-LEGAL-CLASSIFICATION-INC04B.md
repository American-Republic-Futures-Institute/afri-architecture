## Distinctions that must survive projection

1. **Structural source membership** is separate from **statutory status**, **organizational supervision**, **specific functional delegation**, **adjudication**, **audit/investigation independence**, and **fiscal authority**.
2. Sections 402 and 403 provide a general IG baseline; Treasury-specific **§412** conditions and the §412(a)(4) TIGTA exception must be independently represented before making any validated claims about reporting or direction.
3. A regulatory delegation permission is not an executed specific delegation. EOIR regulation §1003.0 names qualified authority and express restrictions; source-record placement is not evidence of individual exercises of that power.
4. Different *as-of* editions can contain amendments and supersession. OLRC code display and eCFR current pages are not signed immutable legal citator packages; subsequent legislation, amendments, rules and controlling judicial interpretations must be reviewed by a qualified person.
5. **No nine gate reviewed/approved**. `controlling_primary_legal_instrument_id`, `statute_subsection_verified`, `explicit_delegation_instrument_verified`, `legal_classification_final` and `independent_reviewer_id` remain unverified/null in the machine-readable model.
