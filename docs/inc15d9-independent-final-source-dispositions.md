## Historical identity and legal-temporal safeguards

**Overseas Private Investment Corporation — 2025 source ID `169`.** This is the source's original historical name, not a legal finding about the existence, identity or scope of a present-day successor. No current alias, transferred duty, dissolved entity, institutional continuity or renamed department is inferred. A separate dated primary source and source-to-canonical adjudication are prerequisites.

**United States Agency for International Development — source ID `180`.** The fact that USAID appears in the 2025 Government Manual does not resolve what legal or operational organization existed at any later point in 2026. The observation cannot be used to assert continuation, dissolution, authority transfer, workforce levels, supervised operations or a current functional interface.

**Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation and Tennessee Valley Authority — source IDs `171`, `178`.** The labels “Corporation” and “Authority” are publisher wording only within this model. Legal corporate form, institutional subtype, ownership, fiscal or appropriations responsibility, governance, and supervisory or delegated control require individually retained law/charter originals and independent review.

All other board, commission, office and agency labels are held as source-only observations. Publisher mission statements are not signed statutory instruments and cannot demonstrate that powers were legally delegated or exercised.
