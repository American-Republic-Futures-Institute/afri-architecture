## Original publisher claims and research limits

Section 3503 of Title 44 reports a **legal requirement to delegate** functions to the OIRA Administrator; it is **not a signed individual execution record**, an authenticated current incumbent, or evidence of successful delegation. Statutorily named offices are never converted to source-described executive management decisions.

The FTC observations follow its [Bureaus and Offices](https://www.ftc.gov/about-ftc/bureaus-offices) directory: Competition, Consumer Protection and Economics Bureaus, plus General Counsel and Secretary offices. [15 U.S.C. §41](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t15/s41/) separately establishes the Commission's collective statutory composition. Statutory commission structure, executive control, presidential appointment, appointment validity, removal limits, administrative review, bureau reporting, enforcement and congressional oversight are **different legal propositions**; current judicial and legal effect are not adjudicated here.

The 1970 OMB reorganization and 1990 statutory section renumbering appear as **publisher-reported historical events** only; they do not create new agency IDs or certify succession. The original source-instrument bytes, complete current rosters, independent statutory/citator review, individual signed delegations, direct supervision, and independently qualified custody remain evidence gaps.
